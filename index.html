☯ Reincarnation Path

Reincarnation is a prestige mechanic. Your normal cultivation returns to Mortal, but your permanent power remains.

Each reincarnation grants a permanent +25% multiplier to cultivation, combat power, and maximum HP. Normal weapons will be lost, but Mythic ones endure.

You must completely max out Immortal Ascension before reincarnating.
